Bhutan is a captivating, landlocked kingdom nestled in the Eastern Himalayas, known for its stunning landscapes, ancient monasteries, and vibrant Buddhist culture. Often called the "Land of the Thunder Dragon," it is globally unique for prioritizing Gross National Happiness over economic wealth, focusing instead on environmental preservation and community well-being.
Bhutan is a beautiful country with rich culture, peaceful people, amazing landscapes, and historic places. It is a special destination for people who want to experience nature and Bhutanese culture.
